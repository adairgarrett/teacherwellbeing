Campus Pilot & District Roadmap

From Hillcrest High School to District-Wide Support

Designed with a modular architecture so Hillcrest High can demonstrate peer mutual aid, with the capacity to coordinate with neighboring schools in the future.

PHASE 1 (ACTIVE)

Hillcrest High School Pilot

Hillcrest High faculty testbed. Validating quick class coverage speed, peer de-escalation, and daily teacher wellbeing practices.

PHASE 2

District Cluster Network

Inter-school support sharing: feeder middle and high schools coordinate prep-block coverage and resource banks.

PHASE 3

Faculty Retention & Evaluation

Anonymized program evaluation metrics providing superintendents with predictive burnout data and intervention efficacy.
